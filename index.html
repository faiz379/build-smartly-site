About us

A share company built by people who build.

Build Smartly S.C. is an Ethiopian share company. Its shareholders are contractors, engineers, suppliers and investors who have all bought materials the hard way: many phone calls, prices that change by the day, and no record of what was agreed.

We are building one place where a contractor sends a list once, verified suppliers quote in writing, and the purchase order and contract are issued with a full record. Addis Ababa first, then the regional cities.

    Questions and answers

    What people ask us

    Does it cost anything to register?

    No. Registration is free for customers and suppliers. A fee applies only when a deal is completed, as a small percentage of the order. The rate will be published before launch.

    Who sees my request?

    Only verified suppliers of the materials on your list. They see a customer number and the sub-city of delivery, never your name or exact address. Your identity appears only in the purchase order.

    Are the prices on the home page live?

    No. They are indicative prices confirmed by suppliers that morning. A binding price comes only as a written offer on your request, with a validity date.

    Can I accept only part of my list?

    Yes. You accept, reject or renegotiate each line separately, and you can split a line between two suppliers if both allow partial supply.

    How do I pay?

    The payment term is agreed per order: advance, on shipping, on delivery, or credit with a date. Payment goes through the platform's payment provider so that both sides have proof.

    Do I need an account to send a request?

    Only your mobile number. Build your list, press Send, type the SMS code. That is the account. Your list is saved on your phone while you do it, so you can leave and come back.

    Which documents do I need?

    Buyers: your name or company name and TIN, asked once when you accept your first offer, so the purchase order can be issued. A person without a TIN shows a Fayda ID to staff instead. A government office gives an official authorisation letter. Suppliers: business licence and TIN certificate before the first quote. Nothing is needed to open the account or to send a request.

    Can I use Telegram instead of the website?

    Yes, this is planned for launch. The Build Smartly bot will let you send your list as a message, a photo or an Excel file, and will send you notifications when offers arrive. The website and the bot share the same account and the same records.

    Where is my data kept?

    On servers in Ethiopia, as required by the Personal Data Protection Proclamation. Your location and documents are private and are never shown on a public profile.

    Terms of use

    Plain-language summary of what the final terms will cover. Ethiopian counsel writes the binding text.

    1. The service

    Build Smartly is a platform that connects verified buyers and suppliers of construction materials and equipment. The sale contract is between the buyer and the supplier. Build Smartly issues the purchase order and contract documents and records the deal.

    2. Accounts and verification

    One account per person or organisation. Suppliers must hold a valid business licence. Documents are checked by staff before trading. Accounts with expired documents are restricted.

    3. Requests and offers

    An offer is binding for its validity period. A buyer who accepts an offer and a supplier who reconfirms it are bound by the purchase order and contract.

    4. Fees

    A percentage of each completed sale, published on the website. No fee for registration or for sending requests.

    5. Hidden information

    Locations, contact details and identities are hidden until the purchase order. Members may not use information from the platform to deal outside it during the protection period.

    6. Disputes

    Delivery shortages, damage and payment issues are reported in the app and handled by Build Smartly support before any other step.

    Privacy notice

    Written under the Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024.

    What we collect

    Name, mobile number, email, company details, licence and tax documents, locations you add, and the records of your requests, offers and orders.

    Why

    To verify members, match requests with suppliers, issue documents, deliver goods and keep the legal records of each deal.

    Who sees it

    Only the staff whose job needs it, and only with a log entry. Other members see your identity only in a purchase order. Nothing is sold or shared with advertisers.

    Where

    On servers in Ethiopia. Cross-border transfers only as the law allows.

    Your rights

    You can see, correct and ask for deletion of your data, subject to legal record-keeping. Write to the contact on the Contact page.

    Start here

    Build Smartly is the working name. Everything works, but no SMS is sent and no money moves. One buyer, two suppliers and two staff logins.

    Watch how it works

    A 6-minute walkthrough with sound (turn your volume up): a buyer sends a request, suppliers quote, the buyer chooses and completes the order, agrees to the contracts, receives the goods and pays; then the staff page and the three languages.

    Your logins

    SMS code for every number: 123456. Supplier portal password: smartly.

    As the buyer
    1. Send a request: tick the materials, type quantities, choose who delivers, Send. Sign in with 0911 000 201 and code 123456.
    2. Both test suppliers receive it, and a few background suppliers quote by themselves within minutes, so each line gets several offers. The bell at the top right lights up.
    3. Open My requests. Each line has its own box, cheapest first. Choose one offer per line, reject, or propose your own price.
    4. Press Complete the order: delivery, insurance and your company details, once. One order per supplier.
    5. Follow each order on its page: agree to the contract, confirm delivery with the handover code, pay. Documents are under Statements.
    As a supplier
    1. Open the Supplier portal with 0911 000 301 or 0911 000 302 and the password smartly.
    2. Price list: tick what you sell, type prices, Save. Quotes are pre-filled from it.
    3. Requests: send your offer per line. The buyer sees your brand, not your name.
    4. Offers and orders: Waiting for your action lists counter-offers to answer and orders to reconfirm, prepare and dispatch.

    Tip: use two browser windows, one as the buyer and one as a supplier.

    Switching login

    Tap your name at the top right, then Switch account. Any other Ethiopian number also works as a new buyer.
